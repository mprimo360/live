📖 How to Test FuelGuard Try all scenarios
⛽ Refuel
Simulates filling the tank. Fuel level rises gradually. System detects it as normal refuelling.
🚨 Theft
Simulates fuel theft while parked. Instant drop, alert sent, camera activates, GPS location recorded.
🔧 Tamper
Simulates sensor cable being cut. Tamper alert triggers, camera captures evidence, backup battery activates.
🚚 Drive
Simulates driving with real-time fuel consumption, speed changes, and GPS movement. Shows normal fuel usage.
📸 Camera
Camera activates automatically during theft or tamper. Captures evidence with timestamp and location.
🔔 Alerts
Real-time notifications via Telegram/WhatsApp style feed. Shows what fleet owners receive instantly.
💡 How It Works: The system uses a fuel-level sensor, GPS, accelerometer, and camera. It distinguishes between normal consumption (driving) and suspicious fuel loss (parked with engine off). When theft is detected, it sends an alert with location, fuel loss amount, and camera evidence.
